# AI service (planned)

Reserved for a future Python/FastAPI service. No OCR, model, or Gemini integration is implemented here.

Potential services include OCR and document extraction, document classification, anomaly detection, explainable risk scoring, land-use change detection, and encroachment indicators. These are decision-support signals only; provide source references, confidence and human review, and do not present a model score as proof of fraud or a legal conclusion.
